GenLayer Intelligent Contract · Sports Vertical · Escrow

TrueStake

A two-party, address-bound, deadline-gated, multi-source sports score settlement contract that also custodies both parties' GEN stakes and pays the winner automatically, on-chain. Party binding, a single-day resolution window after full-time, mandatory corroboration from at least two reputable sports outlets, and a permissionless pull-payment withdrawal — no side ever settles a match on one page, and no GEN moves until a real verdict (or an explicit refund path) says it should.

Accept / Cancel Agreement

Only party_b can accept, and only by sending EXACTLY party_a's stake. Only party_a can cancel (and only before acceptance) — cancelling refunds party_a's stake for withdrawal.

Looks up this agreement's stake_amount on-chain and fills the field above, so you never have to guess or retype it by hand.

Resolve Agreement

Only callable after the deadline (full-time) and before the 24h resolution window closes. Every committed domain (and pinned path) must be represented.

On the FIRST successful attempt, this exact set of URLs is locked for this agreement — every later retry must resubmit the same set.

Expire Agreement

Permissionless. Moves a lapsed agreement (never accepted, or never resolved in time) to "expired" and credits the relevant stake(s) back to their own depositor — party_a only if party_b never accepted, both parties equally if the resolution window closed unresolved.

Withdraw & Balances

Payouts and refunds are never pushed automatically — they only credit an internal balance. withdraw() is the one method that actually moves GEN, and it always sends the caller's own full credited balance (from every agreement combined) to the caller's own wallet.
